# Editor feature surface proven by the submitted IPA

This list is derived from actual `LVEditor.bundle` resources in the submitted CapCut 19.3.0 IPA. The bundle contains 456 SVG control/resource identifiers. The entries below are the editor-relevant feature controls; generic navigation/share icons are omitted.

Presence here proves that the corresponding editor surface exists in this build. It does **not** by itself prove that a required online material/model is embedded; see `CAPCUT_IPA_STATIC_AUDIT.md` for that distinction.

## Core edit / transform

Canvas, keyframes, delete keyframe, blend, crop, overlay/PIP, freeze, graph interpolation, horizontal flip, vertical flip, mirror, opacity, aspect ratio, reverse, rotate, speed, split, split sentence, trim.

## Color / filters

HSL, Adjust, brightness, color match, contrast, curves, exposure, fade, grain, highlights, hue, saturation, shadows, sharpen, temperature, vignette, AI highlight, auto adjust, blacks, brilliance, color wheel, color correction, color picker, filters, whites, presets/templates.

## Cutout / masks

AI remove, AI brush, AI background surface, manual brush, chroma key, customized cutout, cutout intensity, cutout on/off, cutout stroke, masks, erasers, quick brush, quick eraser, remove-filler-word surface and remove-flicker surface.

## Quality / tracking

Auto reframe, camera tracking, custom camera tracking, hand/head tracking controls, general tracking, stabilization, motion blur, optical flow, video/audio noise reduction controls, image enhancement and super-resolution controls.

## Effects / animation

AI effects, body effects, photo effects, video effects, transitions, animations, stickers, object effects, relight, velocity effects.

## Audio / voice

AI sound effects, AI music, add voice, audio effects, extract audio, audio fade, local files, record, sounds, voice clone, beat controls, voice changer, enhance voice, vocal isolation, vocal polish and voiceover.

## Text / captions

Add captions, add text, AI text templates, auto captions, bilingual captions, caption AI highlight, caption templates, multi-select captions, text animations, text effects, fonts, bubbles, drawing, alignment, text style, text templates, text-to-audio, text-to-speech, text-to-image, text-to-video, text tracking and transcript/text-based editing.

## AI surfaces

AI movement, AI replace, AI avatar, AI background, AI character/customize character, AI cut, AI dubbing, AI expression, AI material, AI MV, AI translator, AI writer, lip sync, outpaint and overdub.
